RIFT BESTIARY

Nearby rifts spawn monsters at different ranks. The stronger the rank, the greater the danger they pose. Monsters far more powerful than you will present a serious challenge, while those closer to your own rank may provide a more manageable encounter. Learn to recognise the signs of a dangerous Rift before you step through.

⚔️ YOUR FIRST ENCOUNTER

How to fight a Rift monster

1. Choose your abilitySelect an ability from the list. Each ability uses a different movement or exercise.
2. Press START MOVEYour attack begins and you start dealing damage to the monster.
3. Keep movingThe more you move during your attack, the more damage you will deal.
4. Stop when you choosePress STOP ATTACK at any point to end your attack early.
5. Recover your vitalityAfter combat, hold Mountain Pose to recover your health.
